Foundations

Three colours, one job each. Blue is the calm structure: top bar, sidebar, page bands. Butter is the highlight: the one thing that matters most on a screen. Chocopie is the ink and the action: text, icons, primary buttons.

The one decorative motif: thin horizontal stripes, borrowed from the tower floors and the awning in the mark. Blue stripes on Blue, butter stripes on Butter. Used behind sign-in, the main card and empty states only.

48

Every touch target is at least 48 × 48 px. Customer screens are one column, 480 px at most.

Resident app

Phone first, one column, one main action per screen. Shown here as Asha, flat A-101, Green Meadows Apartments. Each frame is a different moment in the same month.

Store workspace

Desktop first, with packing usable on a phone. Shown as Ravi of Sri Lakshmi Stores. A store sees only its own stock, orders and collections, and never a resident's phone number.

Building admin

Shown as Meera, coordinator for Green Meadows Apartments. Two lists matter: who has paid and can be delivered to, and who still needs a reminder. Admins never mark anything as paid.
